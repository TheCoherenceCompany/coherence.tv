1. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how The Gathering LDA, trading as The Coherence Company (“The Coherence Company”, “we”, “us” or “our”), collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data in connection with our internal LinkedIn publishing application (the “Application”).

The Application allows authorised members of our team to connect to LinkedIn and publish approved content on The Coherence Company’s LinkedIn Page.

The Application is currently an internal business tool. It is not offered to customers, members of the public or third-party organisations.

This Privacy Policy applies only to personal data processed through the Application. Other websites, products and services operated by The Gathering LDA may be covered by separate privacy notices.

This notice is intended to provide the information required under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, including information about the controller, processing purposes, lawful bases, recipients, retention periods and individual rights.

3. What the Application does

The Application may be used to:

  • authenticate authorised members of The Coherence Company team;
  • connect the Application to The Coherence Company’s LinkedIn Page;
  • confirm that a user has permission to administer or publish to that Page;
  • prepare, review, schedule and publish content;
  • transmit text, images, videos, documents, links and other approved materials to LinkedIn;
  • record whether a publication request succeeded or failed;
  • maintain an operational history of publishing activity;
  • diagnose technical errors;
  • protect the Application and LinkedIn Page from unauthorised access; and
  • comply with LinkedIn’s developer and API requirements.

The Application is not designed to monitor LinkedIn members, scrape LinkedIn data, build advertising profiles or collect information about people who interact with our LinkedIn Page.

4. Personal data we process

The personal data we process depends on how the Application is configured and used.

4.1 Authorised-user information

We may process:

  • your name;
  • your work email address;
  • your job title or role;
  • your relationship with The Gathering LDA;
  • an internal user or account identifier;
  • your LinkedIn member identifier;
  • confirmation that you administer or are authorised to publish to our LinkedIn Page;
  • your Application permissions;
  • the date your access was granted, changed or removed; and
  • records of actions carried out through the Application.

4.2 LinkedIn authentication and authorisation information

When an authorised user connects the Application to LinkedIn, we may receive or store:

  • OAuth access tokens;
  • refresh tokens, where available;
  • token creation and expiry information;
  • permissions or scopes granted to the Application;
  • LinkedIn organisation or Page identifiers;
  • authentication status;
  • authorisation and revocation records; and
  • technical information required to maintain the connection.

OAuth tokens allow the Application to perform authorised actions without collecting or storing the user’s LinkedIn password.

We do not ask for or store LinkedIn passwords.

4.3 Publication content

We may process content prepared, uploaded or submitted through the Application, including:

  • post text;
  • captions;
  • article text;
  • images;
  • graphics;
  • audio or video;
  • documents;
  • links and associated metadata;
  • publication dates and scheduling information;
  • draft, approval and publication status;
  • the identity of the person who submitted, reviewed or approved the content;
  • internal comments about the content;
  • publication history; and
  • identifiers returned by LinkedIn for published posts.

Publication content may contain personal data where it identifies or depicts an individual.

Anyone submitting content through the Application must ensure that the content has been authorised for publication and that any permissions, consents, releases or licences required for its use have been obtained.

4.4 Technical, activity and security information

We may process technical and operational information such as:

  • the date and time the Application was accessed;
  • authentication and login events;
  • publication attempts and outcomes;
  • error messages;
  • changes to permissions or connections;
  • IP addresses;
  • browser information;
  • device and operating-system information;
  • server and application logs;
  • security alerts;
  • records of suspected misuse;
  • records of unauthorised-access attempts; and
  • information concerning technical or security incidents.

We collect only information that is reasonably necessary to operate, maintain and protect the Application.

5. Information we do not intentionally collect

Through the Application, we do not intend to collect:

  • LinkedIn passwords;
  • private LinkedIn messages;
  • LinkedIn contact lists or personal connections;
  • the contents of personal LinkedIn accounts;
  • visitor information relating to personal LinkedIn profiles;
  • information about individual followers of our Page;
  • personal data contained in comments or reactions;
  • LinkedIn advertising or campaign data;
  • sensitive or special-category personal data;
  • information about criminal convictions or offences;
  • children’s personal data; or
  • information for behavioural advertising or commercial profiling.

We do not use the Application to scrape, reconstruct or reproduce LinkedIn’s services.

We do not sell, rent or commercially trade personal data or information obtained through LinkedIn.

LinkedIn requires API users to minimise the data they request and limits how certain LinkedIn data may be stored and displayed.

6. How we obtain personal data

We may obtain personal data:

  • directly from authorised users;
  • from The Gathering LDA’s internal administrators;
  • from LinkedIn after an authorised user grants access through LinkedIn’s OAuth authorisation process;
  • from content submitted for publication;
  • from our hosting, authentication and security systems;
  • automatically through technical and operational logs; and
  • from correspondence relating to support, security or privacy requests.

LinkedIn separately determines how it processes information through its platform, accounts and authorisation services. LinkedIn’s processing is governed by its own privacy documentation and user settings.

  • investigate misuse;

  • maintain security logs;

8. Our lawful bases for processing

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on one or more of the following lawful bases.

8.1 Legitimate interests

We process personal data where necessary for our legitimate interests in:

  • managing The Coherence Company’s public communications;
  • operating an efficient internal publishing process;
  • managing authorised access;
  • protecting our LinkedIn Page;
  • securing our technical systems;
  • maintaining accurate operational records; and
  • preventing and investigating misuse.

Before relying on legitimate interests, we consider whether the processing is necessary and whether the interests or fundamental rights of the individual override our interests.

The Application is limited to authorised internal users, and we seek to process only the information reasonably necessary for its operation.

8.2 Performance of a contract

Where an authorised user is an employee, contractor, consultant or service provider, certain processing may be necessary to perform or manage their employment, consultancy or services agreement with us.

8.3 Compliance with a legal obligation

We may process personal data where necessary to comply with legal obligations that apply to The Gathering LDA.

8.4 Consent

An authorised user grants LinkedIn permissions through LinkedIn’s OAuth authorisation process.

Where consent is the appropriate legal basis under data-protection law, an individual may withdraw that consent at any time.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

9. LinkedIn permissions and restrictions

The Application requests only the LinkedIn permissions reasonably necessary to authenticate authorised users and publish approved content to The Coherence Company’s LinkedIn Page.

We do not use LinkedIn information obtained through the Application to:

  • sell personal data;
  • create advertising or behavioural profiles;
  • carry out unrelated marketing;
  • monitor LinkedIn members;
  • score or rank individuals;
  • combine LinkedIn data with unrelated datasets for profiling;
  • make decisions about employment, credit, insurance, housing or eligibility;
  • infer sensitive information;
  • scrape LinkedIn content;
  • reproduce LinkedIn’s databases;
  • circumvent LinkedIn’s access controls;
  • provide LinkedIn data to unauthorised third parties; or
  • train public or general-purpose artificial-intelligence models.

Where LinkedIn information is obtained for the management of The Coherence Company’s Page, it will be accessible only to people authorised to manage, operate or technically support that Page.

LinkedIn imposes restrictions on the storage and display of information obtained through its APIs, including short storage periods for some categories of member information. The Application will follow the applicable LinkedIn storage requirement where it is shorter than the retention period stated in this policy.

10. AI-assisted content

Content published through the Application may have been drafted, edited, translated, summarised or reviewed using artificial-intelligence tools.

Where AI-assisted content is used:

  • an authorised human must review and approve it before publication;
  • AI-generated output must not be assumed to be accurate;
  • factual statements should be checked before publication;
  • confidential or sensitive information must not be entered into an AI service unless that service has been approved for such use;
  • the person approving the post remains responsible for the publication decision; and
  • LinkedIn personal data obtained through the Application will not be used to train public or general-purpose AI models.

The Application does not use LinkedIn member data for automated profiling or decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

14. Disconnecting LinkedIn

An authorised user may revoke the Application’s access through LinkedIn’s account or application settings.

An authorised user may also contact us to request that we:

  • disconnect their LinkedIn authorisation;
  • invalidate or delete stored access tokens;
  • remove their access to the Application;
  • delete personal data associated with their use of the Application; or
  • delete unpublished content they submitted.

Requests should be sent to: team@coherence.tv

When an authorised user leaves The Gathering LDA or no longer requires access:

  • their Application access will be withdrawn;
  • applicable tokens will be deleted or invalidated;
  • their permission to schedule or publish content will end;
  • information no longer required will be deleted or anonymised; and
  • limited records may be retained where necessary for security, legal compliance or accountability.

Deleting information from the Application does not automatically remove content that has already been published on LinkedIn.

Published content must be removed separately through LinkedIn or through the Application where that function is supported.
